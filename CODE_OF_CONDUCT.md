# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Team Agent contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make participation respectful and productive. Examples include:

- communicating with empathy and technical clarity;
- welcoming different experience levels and perspectives;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy, credentials, project data, and personal boundaries;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other material that violates this standard. Responses may include a warning, temporary participation limits, or permanent exclusion, depending on impact and pattern of behavior.

Report public problems using GitHub's report controls. For a privacy-sensitive report, open a [private advisory](https://github.com/boxzeemon-beep/team-agent/security/advisories/new) with `[Conduct]` at the start of the title. Maintainers will limit access to the people needed to review the report and will avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces officially associated with Team Agent.
